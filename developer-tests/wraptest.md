# Wrapping test

The PcW16 was Amstrad's last word processor, a small grey machine with a flash disc and a floppy drive. Its software, Rosanne, draws every character in proportion, so a row of narrow letters holds many more characters than a row of wide ones, and an editor that wraps by counting characters leaves ragged gaps or runs off the edge. MDEDIT IV measures each row in pixels instead.
